About Us
Where every dancer can bloom.

At Bloom Dance Studio we offer a lifelong dance education, from childhood through adolescence into adulthood. The journey begins with a simple intention: to create an environment where dancers fall in love with dance. The love of dance is what draws them back through the studio door, week after week, year after year. Over time, discipline and devotion deepen, technical excellence takes root, and every dancer has the opportunity to flourish.

What we offer
Classes for all
Mini & Me
18 MONTHS TO 3 YEARS OLD

This is a gentle first step into dance class that is shared with a parent, guardian or loved one. Through creative play, you explore musicality and the basic foundations of dance with your little one. A time to bond, be a bit silly, and most importantly, encourage their budding love of dance.

Little Blooms
2 to 5 years old

Inspired by the RAD Dance to Your Own Tune framework, these classes use imaginative storytelling to develop vital social and physical skills. Children develop motor control, balance, coordination, and spatial awareness while learning to interpret rhythm and tempo. Prioritising creative physical discovery over rigid form from the outset, these classes build a solid base for all future dance training.

Ballet
RAD Pre-Primary to Advanced 2

Our ballet classes follow the Royal Academy of Dance syllabus, an internationally recognised curriculum that has set the standard for classical training for over a century. With a focus on building confidence, technical artistry and performance quality, we follow a pace that suits each dancer while giving them the opportunity to take part in assessments as well as festivals, competitions and shows.

Contemporary
Children and Adults

A natural partner to ballet, contemporary dance is expressive, grounded and versatile. Classes explore floor work, release and momentum, the use of breath and weight, all building strength and fluidity while giving each dancer room to find their own way of moving. Those who wish can perform contemporary work at festivals, competitions and concerts.

A dancer, like a flower, does not bloom in a moment but through determination, constant reaching for light.

The people behind the magic
Teaching Team
Kim Taylor

As founder and studio principal since 2018, Kim serves as your primary contact. Bringing a strong academic and creative streak, she pairs qualifications in RAD Ballet and AIDT Modern dance with a university background in physical theatre, psychology, and marketing. Her teaching philosophy centres on play, prioritising creativity, musicality and body positivity from an early age.

Ipeleng Derby

Ipeleng joined the studio in 2020, teaching ballet and contemporary for all ages across multiple venues. A graduate of the Cape Academy of Performing Arts, she has toured globally with the Cape Dance Company and Dada Masilo, and sits on the board of Cape Ballet Africa. Qualified in RAD Ballet and completing her Pilates certification, she pairs elite performance experience with a warm and encouraging teaching style.

Joanne Dieckmann

Joanne joined the studio in 2020, bringing more than three decades of dance education experience to the team. Mentored by Yvonne Adkins (ARAD) of the Royal Ballet School, she has taught the full RAD syllabus from Pre-Primary through Advanced 2 since 1987, alongside credentials in the Vaganova-based BC programme and AIDT Modern. A recipient of the AAB Performance Award for 25 years of service, she focuses on senior students, balancing technical discipline with helping dancers discover their artistic voice.

Pascale Verolini

Pascale joined the team in 2026. She has worked as a locum RAD teacher for Bloom, and her primary focus is now studio administrative support. Backed by international teaching experience across South Africa and the UAE, and Southdowns College Dance Honours training, she brings first-hand syllabus knowledge and dancer-led insight to our day-to-day operations.

Rorisang Motuba

Rorisang joined the team in 2026 to lead children’s creative movement across our partner preschools. A lifelong friend of Kim, she is a formally trained actor, writer, and director who studied at UCT, graduated magna cum laude from the New York Film Academy, and trained at the Baron/Brown Studio. Drawing on lead roles at the Market Theatre, she brings professional expertise in spatial awareness, physical expression, and storytelling to young performers.

Tamlyn Preston

Joining the studio in 2026, Tamlyn began her training in Johannesburg before heading to the UK to study at KS Dance, going on to perform across Europe with Étoile Ballet Theatre in Italy and EBB Junior Ballet in France. Holder of the Enrico Cecchetti Final Diploma and an ISTD Diploma in Dance Education, she draws on her stage experience to help dancers of every level find confidence, precision and joy in both ballet and contemporary.
